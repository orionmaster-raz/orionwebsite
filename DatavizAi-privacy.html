Privacy Policy for DataViz AI Chrome Extension

This policy explains how DataViz AI collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use the DataViz AI Chrome Extension.

Last Updated: January 2, 2025 Applies to: DataViz AI Chrome Extension

Introduction

DataViz AI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Chrome extension ("Extension" or "Service").

By using the Extension, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

1. User-Provided Information

  • Email Address: When you sign in or subscribe, we collect your email address to manage your account and subscription.
  • Payment Information: Payment processing is handled securely by Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers or payment details. We only receive the last 4 digits of your card for verification purposes.
  • Screenshots and Images: When you use the "Select Area" feature or upload documents, you provide images containing tables, charts, formulas, or other data for processing.

2. Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you use the Extension, including:
    • Number of extractions performed
    • Types of data extracted (tables, charts, formulas)
    • Subscription plan status
    • Feature usage patterns
  • Device Information: We may collect information about your device, including:
    • Browser type and version
    • Extension version
    • Operating system
  • Local Storage Data: The Extension stores the following data locally on your device:
    • User authentication state
    • Subscription plan information
    • Extraction history
    • User preferences
    • Free usage count

3. Website Content

When you use the "Select Area" feature, the Extension captures screenshots of the content you select from web pages. This content is sent to our servers for AI processing and is not stored permanently.

4. User Activity

During the area selection process, the Extension monitors mouse clicks and cursor position to enable the selection tool. This data is used only during the active selection process and is not stored.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  1. Service Provision: To provide, maintain, and improve the Extension's functionality, including:
    • Processing images to extract data
    • Converting data to CSV format
    • Providing AI-powered explanations of formulas and equations
    • Managing your subscription and account
  2. Communication: To send you:
    • Service-related notifications
    • Processing status updates
    • Important updates about the Extension
  3. Analytics: To understand how users interact with the Extension and improve our services.
  4. Security: To detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security threats.

Data Processing and AI Services

Image Processing

When you submit images for analysis, we send them to our secure backend servers, which use Google's Gemini AI service to:

  • Extract tabular data from screenshots
  • Extract data points from charts and graphs
  • Analyze and explain mathematical formulas
  • Process PDF documents

Important:

  • Images are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored on our servers.
  • Images are only sent when you explicitly trigger an extraction.
  • All communication is encrypted via HTTPS.

Third-Party AI Services

We use Google's Gemini AI API for image analysis. When you submit images, they are sent to Google's servers for processing. Google's use of your data is governed by their privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Storage

Local Storage

The Extension stores data locally on your device using Chrome's storage API:

  • User authentication tokens
  • Subscription status
  • Extraction history
  • User preferences

This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers except for authentication and subscription verification.

Server Storage

We store the following information on our secure servers:

  • User account information (email address)
  • Subscription status and plan details
  • Usage statistics (anonymized)
  • Processing logs (temporarily, for debugging purposes)

Data Retention

  • Images: Images sent for processing are not permanently stored. They are deleted after processing is complete.
  • Account Data: We retain your account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services.
  • Usage Data: We retain anonymized usage statistics for analytics purposes.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  1. Service Providers: We share data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the Extension:
    • Stripe: For payment processing (payment data is handled directly by Stripe)
    • Google Gemini AI: For AI-powered image analysis
    • Hosting Providers: For secure server infrastructure
  2. Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.
  3. Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Secure Storage: User data is stored on secure servers with access controls
  • Payment Security: Payment processing is handled by Stripe, which is PCI DSS compliant
  • Regular Updates: We regularly update our security practices and infrastructure

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

  1. Access: You can access your account information through the Extension's settings.
  2. Correction: You can update your account information at any time.
  3. Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.
  4. Data Portability: You can request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  5. Opt-Out: You can stop using the Extension at any time and uninstall it from your browser.
  6. Subscription Management: You can cancel your subscription at any time through the Extension or by contacting us.
